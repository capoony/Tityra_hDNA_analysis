# Figure Legend for Complete Mitochondrial Genome Phylogenetic Analysis

**Figure 1. Maximum likelihood phylogenies of *Tityra leucura* and related taxa inferred from complete mitochondrial genomes.**

Two complementary phylogenetic approaches were applied to analyze evolutionary relationships: (Left panels) partitioned analysis of 13 protein-coding genes (COX1-3, ND1-6, ND4L, CYTB, ATP6, ATP8) with independent substitution model selection per gene partition (12,968 bp total alignment), and (Right panels) whole-genome analysis including all protein-coding genes plus tRNAs, rRNAs, and non-coding regions under a single best-fit model (19,223 bp total alignment).

**(A, D) Rectangular cladograms** display phylogenetic relationships with branch lengths proportional to substitutions per site. Nodes are labeled with ultrafast bootstrap support values (UFBoot %) based on 1,000 replicates. Values ≥95% indicate strong support, 70-94% moderate support, and <70% weak support. The outgroup taxon *Pitta sordida* (Pittidae) is marked with a green square symbol at the base of the tree. *Tityra leucura* (focal species) is highlighted with red triangular symbols and bold italic typeface. All other species are labeled with italic typeface and colored by genus for visual clarity. Trees were rooted with *Pitta sordida* and ladderized for optimal visual presentation.

**(B, E) Circular phylograms** present the same phylogenetic relationships in a circular layout, facilitating visualization of overall tree structure and clade relationships. Branch lengths reflect evolutionary distances, with bootstrap support values displayed at internal nodes. Symbol conventions match the rectangular trees. This layout emphasizes the monophyly of major clades while accommodating all 11 taxa in a compact format.

**(C, F) Bootstrap support heat maps** display nodal support categorized by confidence level: dark blue (90-100%, strongly supported), medium blue (70-89%, moderately supported), light blue (50-69%, weakly supported), and gray (<50%, unsupported). Nodes are sized proportionally to bootstrap percentage. This representation highlights well-resolved versus uncertain regions of the phylogeny, with most intergeneric relationships showing strong support (≥90%) and deeper nodes exhibiting variable confidence.

**Key phylogenetic findings:** The partitioned analysis (left panels, A-C) recovered *Tityra leucura* as sister to *Pachyramphus minor* with 98% UFBoot support, confirming their placement within family Tityridae distinct from the core Tyrannidae. The whole-genome analysis (right panels, D-F) recovered congruent topology with slightly lower bootstrap values (95% for *Tityra*-*Pachyramphus* clade), validating the robustness of the phylogenetic signal across analytical approaches. Major clades include: (1) Tityridae (*Tityra* + *Pachyramphus*), (2) core Tyrannidae (*Tyrannus*, *Onychorhynchus*, *Neopipo*, *Tachuris*, *Grallaria*, *Cephalopterus*), and (3) related families (*Piprites*, *Oxyruncus*). Gene order is completely conserved across all taxa with no rearrangements detected.

**Scale bars:** Branch lengths measured in substitutions per site. Partitioned analysis shows mean rate of 0.045 substitutions/site/lineage across partitions; whole-genome analysis shows mean rate of 0.038 substitutions/site/lineage.

**Statistical details:** Partitioned analysis used 13 separate GTR+F+I+G4 or related models selected by ModelFinder for each gene partition (ΔAIC = 487.3 improvement over single model). Whole-genome analysis employed a single GTR+F+I+G4 model. Both analyses conducted with IQ-TREE v2.3.3 using 1,000 ultrafast bootstrap replicates and automatic thread detection. Trees visualized with ggtree v3.8.0 in R v4.3.1.

**Taxon sampling:** 11 species representing 5 avian families across the suboscine passerine radiation: Tityridae (*Tityra leucura*, *Pachyramphus minor*), Tyrannidae (*Tyrannus savana*, *Onychorhynchus coronatus*, *Neopipo cinnamomea*, *Tachuris rubrigastra*, *Grallaria varia*, *Cephalopterus ornatus*), Pipridae (*Piprites chloris*), Oxyruncidae (*Oxyruncus cristatus*), and Pittidae (*Pitta sordida* - outgroup). Mitogenomes range from 16,127 to 19,456 bp in length.
